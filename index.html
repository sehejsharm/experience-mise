Mise is care.

Mise is the service execution platform (SEP) for hotels. It does not write your standards: it implements the standards your hotel already has, running them as timed tasks on staff phones in every department, capturing photo and supervisor evidence as the work happens, and building an audit-ready service record. Not an LMS. No PMS integration required.
