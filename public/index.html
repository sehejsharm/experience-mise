01

You don't own your standards. Your staff do.

Every hotel's standard lives in someone's head. Every resignation quietly deletes a piece of it. It never shows up in a report. It shows up in a review, months later, when it's too late to fix.

02

Right now, someone untrained is serving your best guest. Nobody knows who.

Every shift is a bet that the training stuck. Nobody checks the odds. The guest does, in public, with a star rating attached.

03

If you can't prove it, you can't price it.

Inspectors trust evidence. Guests pay for certainty. A hotel with no service record is asking both to take its word for it. Fewer do every year.
